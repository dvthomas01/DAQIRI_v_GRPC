Gate 3: RoCE end to end, PXIe-8881 -> DGX Spark
================================================
Date        : 2026-08-19
Client      : NI-PXIe-8881-31F6D74, rocep117s0, 192.168.20.2, GID index 5 (RoCE v2 / IPv4)
Server      : spark-ac69,           rocep1s0f0,  192.168.20.1, GID index 3 (RoCE v2 / IPv4)
Link        : 50000 Mb/s, Ethernet, through a switch (not a direct cable)
perftest    : built from source on the PXI into /home/admin/perftest, nothing installed
              system-wide. PCIe relax order: OFF (detected, not stubbed).

Direction matches the real design: the PXI is the client and does the RDMA write, the
Spark is the server and receives it.

Measured twice on purpose. The PXI came up with netdev MTU 1500, giving RoCE active_mtu
1024, while the Spark sat at netdev 9000 / active_mtu 4096. A queue pair silently
negotiates the minimum. Rather than fix that and measure once, the baseline was recorded
first so the MTU effect is attributable on its own.

DELTA
-----
 2 B write, t_typical    1.81 us   -> 1.82 us       no change (this is the control)
 4 MB write, t_typical   730.29 us -> 694.76 us     -35.53 us, 4.87% faster
 4 MB write bandwidth    5518 MiB/s-> 5843 MiB/s    +324.9 MiB/s, +5.89%

The 2-byte row is the control and it is the reason the 4 MB numbers can be trusted. MTU
cannot affect a message that fits in a single packet either way, and it did not move.
Had it moved, the 4 MB improvement would have been drift between runs rather than the
MTU change.


WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE DESIGN
-------------------------------
5843 MiB/s is 6.13 GB/s, or 49.0 Gb/s on a 50 Gb link: about 98% of line rate. The
transport is not where the remaining time is. Small-message latency is 1.81 us typical
with a standard deviation of 0.00 and a 99.9th percentile of 2.20 us, so the fabric is
clean rather than marginal, and there is no fabric configuration problem to solve before
application code exists.

Everything still on the table is in what happens after the bytes land, which is exactly
what the cudaHostAlloc receive-buffer design changes. The 695 us transfer is paid
identically by DAQiri, so it cancels out of the comparison; the 10.94 us registration
penalty measured in handoff section 7c does not.
